Global HIV Trends Analysis

This was a unique project because it had a lot of data recovered from various countries, like the United Kingdom, South Africa etc. We discovered through thorough analysis that there are a chunning rate of deaths from major countries in the world due to HIV-related deaths being heavily concentrated in a small number of high-burden countries, even as global treatment outcomes improved. This Analysis was performed using Power BI.

Green Valley Medical Center

This report evaluates a dataset of 400 patient records spanning six departments, with the objective of assessing recovery outcomes, treatment costs, and length of hospital stay. It further examines performance variations across departments, physicians, treatment types, and patient age groups to identify key trends and insights using Microsoft Excel. This data-driven approach supports improved patient care, enhances operational efficiency, and enables more informed, evidence-based decision-making.
